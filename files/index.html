What is HTML?

HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. It is the language that gives every web page its structure: headings, paragraphs, links, images and more. Browsers read your HTML and turn it into the page you see.

<h1>Hello, world!</h1>
<p>This is my first web page.</p>
